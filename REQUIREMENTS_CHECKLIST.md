- [ ] Re-verify vendor pricing/security data immediately before publishing
- [ ] Deploy the built site to the team's GitHub Pages, Vercel, or AIT-controlled hosting account
- [ ] Prepare/give the 15-minute live presentation to company leadership

The final three unchecked items require current external verification, access to the team's hosting account, or the actual live presentation; they cannot be completed solely inside the source-code package.
